# Legal update 2 draft: limit on absorbed Hosted AI (Terms §6.4) and new yearly plan counts (Terms §6.6)

Draft, 2026-11-20. **Approved and live** on 2026-11-21 (tabula-sites main 8692941). Do not copy this text to the live pages until the owner and a lawyer approve it.

## Why this update is needed

On 2026-11-20 the owner added a stopgap to the absorb rule. We still never take extra AI from the user's balance. But hosted AI for one debate round now has limits, so a bug or a phone left recording cannot run up cost without end. The stopgap:

- We absorb at most a set amount of extra Hosted AI in one debate round.
- Hosted AI for a debate round stops 3 hours after the first speech.
- Hosted AI for a debate round stops after 20 minutes with no new speech and no user action. A tap brings it back if the other limits allow it.
- After a limit, the debate round continues in free mode: free transcription and the free tools. The ballot draft and decision draft are held back, so they still run once.
- No limit ever starts or charges another Round.

The live Terms §6.4 promise "we cover the extra" and "The core of a debate round always finishes", with no limit. Those words must change before checkout goes live.

## Terms §6.4. Long debate rounds

**Before (live text, in full)**

> **6.4 Long debate rounds.** The core of a debate round always finishes. If a very long debate round needs more Hosted AI for the core than one Round holds, we cover the extra. The core of one debate round never uses more than one Round. A debate round is never cut off. Only extra features can use another Round, and only if you say yes (see 6.2).

**After (in full)**

> **6.4 Long debate rounds and limits.** If a very long debate round needs more Hosted AI for the core than one Round holds, we cover the extra, up to a limit. The core of one debate round never uses more than one Round. Only extra features can use another Round, and only if you say yes (see 6.2).
>
> To protect the Service, Hosted AI for one debate round has limits:
>
> - a limit on the extra Hosted AI that we cover;
> - a time limit, counted from when the first speech is recorded; and
> - an idle limit: Hosted AI pauses after a period with no new speech and no action from you. You can turn it back on with a tap, if the other limits allow it.
>
> The app shows the current limits. We may change them. A change never uses another Round or charges you.
>
> When a limit is reached, the debate round continues with the free tools in the app. The clock, flow and ballot keep working, and the ballot draft and decision draft still run once. A limit never uses another Round and never charges you. A debate round is never cut off. [LAWYER L1] [LAWYER L2]

## Terms §6.6. All-Access Round count

Added 2026-11-20. The owner changed the yearly plans: "New: $59 and $149".

| Plan | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Season plan | $79 a year, 70 Rounds | **$59 a year, 60 Rounds** |
| All-Access plan | $149 a year, 135 Rounds | **$149 a year, 165 Rounds** |

The Terms and the Refund Policy do not state the Season price. They say only "a set number of Rounds" (Terms §6). So only one sentence changes. It now also says that all Season Rounds are granted at the start of the term, the same as All-Access. The owner chose this (MODEL.md §D3.6, Option A). The live text says it only for All-Access. The Plans page and checkout show the prices and counts.

**Before (live text, in full)**

> - Season plan and All-Access plan Rounds expire at the end of the 12-month term in which they were granted. All 135 All-Access Rounds are granted at the start of the term.

**After (in full)**

> - Season plan and All-Access plan Rounds expire at the end of the 12-month term in which they were granted. All 60 Season plan Rounds and all 165 All-Access plan Rounds are granted at the start of the term. [LAWYER L4] [LAWYER L6]

**Other option, for the lawyer (L4):** remove the number, so a later count change does not need a Terms change:

Publish this change on the same day as the Plans page update. The marketing session holds the Plans page until then.

No other section changes. The Refund Policy does not change.
