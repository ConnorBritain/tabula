"Paid Plan" means a Single Round, a Round Pack, a Weekend Pass, a Camp Pass, a Subscription or a Program, as described in Section 6.

**§6.1 (add after the Weekend Pass item):**
**Camp Pass.** A one-time purchase of a set number of Rounds to use in 30 days. The 30 days start when the first Round from the pass is committed (see 6.3). We sell the Camp Pass only in a summer sale period each year. The Plans page shows the dates. A Camp Pass does not renew.

**§6.5 (replace the first two sentences; keep the rest):**
We use your Rounds in this order: first Weekend Pass Rounds and Rounds from a Camp Pass that has started, then Subscription Rounds, then Single Round and Round Pack Rounds, then Rounds from a Camp Pass that has not started. Inside each group, we use the Rounds that expire soonest first. A Camp Pass starts when its first Round is committed (see 6.1).

**§6.6 (add after the Weekend Pass item):**
Camp Pass Rounds expire 30 days after the first Round from the pass is committed. If you never commit a Round from the pass, its Rounds expire 12 months after the date of purchase.
