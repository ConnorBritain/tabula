# Debate format times

Speech and prep times used by the format files in formats/.

Times are in minutes. "CX" is cross-examination.

| | **Lincoln-Douglas** (`formats/ld.v1.js`) | **Public Forum** (`formats/pf.v1.js`) |
|---|---|---|
| Speeches | AC 6 · CX 3 · NC 7 · CX 3 · 1AR 4 · NR 6 · 2AR 3 | Constructive 4, 4 · Crossfire 3 · Rebuttal 4, 4 · Crossfire 3 · Summary 3, 3 · Grand Crossfire 3 · Final Focus 2, 2 |
| Prep | 4 per debater | 3 per team |

Public Forum ballots give the win and speaker points, with no speaker ranks (lay judges are common). `formats/pf.v1.js` cites this.

Rule sources: NSDA Lincoln-Douglas event page; NSDA Public Forum event page.
