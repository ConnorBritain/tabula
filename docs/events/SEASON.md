# Tabula Speech Events: season rhythm and the split between the two apps (draft 1)

Status: plan for owner approval, 2026-11-21. No code changed.

This file answers the owner's question of 2026-11-21 about "what happens in the fall versus what happens in the spring", Friday versus Saturday, and whether LD is "a Friday event during the spring, which would technically make it a speech event rather than debate".

Full research, with every source and a confidence note per row: shared notes folder, `speech-events/season-rhythm-research.md` (2026-11-21). Tabroom pages now need a login, so the invitational schedules below come from search summaries of those pages (marked "secondary").

## 1. Short answer

- **The high school year has one long arc.** Invitationals run from September to March. Qualifying tournaments (NSDA districts, state series) run from January to April. State finals run from January (debate in some states) to May (speech in most states). NSDA Nationals is in mid-June. NCFL Grand Nationals is on Memorial Day weekend.
- **"Fall versus spring" is a state fact, not an event fact.** In some states debate ends in winter and speech ends in spring. In others, one combined meet holds everything.
- **"Friday versus Saturday" is a tournament fact, not an event fact.** "Debate and Congress start Friday, speech runs Saturday" is common, but many tournaments do the opposite or put all of it on one day.
- **LD as a spring event holds in Texas UIL, Oklahoma and Missouri.** It does not hold on the national circuit, at NSDA, or in most other states (§3).
- **LD as a speech event holds nowhere.** No league judges LD like a speech event. It keeps two sides, a resolution, cross-examination, prep time and a win-or-loss ballot.
- **So the split between the two apps follows the shape of the room, not the calendar.** Debate events stay in Tabula Debate. Speech events and Congress go in Speech Events. The calendar becomes data that each app reads (§4).

**Plain verdict:**

- **The calendar part holds in Texas UIL, Oklahoma and Missouri.** In UIL, LD qualifies in spring next to Prose, Poetry and Extemp, while CX ends in March and Congress in January. In Missouri, Friday is debate day at state, but for every debate format, not only LD.
- **The calendar part does not hold** on the national circuit, at NSDA, or in Minnesota, Iowa, Indiana, Montana, South Dakota, TFA, CHSSA or NCFL.
- **The "so it is a speech event" part holds nowhere.** The judge in an LD room still flows two sides and picks a winner. That is a Tabula Debate room.

**Could not verify:** the UIL 2026-27 spring dates (UIL says they change this year); Nebraska, Wisconsin and Illinois debate state months; whether Kansas has state LD; NCFL day layout; the NSDA 2026 Nationals day labels.

## 4. The logical breaks: how events split between the two apps

**Rule: an event belongs to the app whose room shape it has.**

| Room shape | App | Events |
|---|---|---|
| Two sides, a resolution or motion, a flow, a win-or-loss ballot | **Tabula Debate** | Policy, LD, PF, World Schools, Big Questions, Extemp Debate, parli |
| Many speakers, one at a time, ranks (and maybe ratings) | **Speech Events** | All interp, public address and limited-prep events |
| A chamber of many students, many short speeches on a docket, per-speech points, top-N ranks | **Speech Events** (Congress grid, [MODELS.md](MODELS.md) §4.6) | Congress (House, Senate) |

Why not split by season or day:

1. **The judge's tool depends on the room, not the date.** A UIL LD round in April and a TOC LD round in October need the same flow and ballot.
2. **A season split would differ by state.** The same LD round would belong to Speech Events in Texas UIL and to Debate in Minnesota. That breaks the "one event, one tool" promise.
3. **Day splits are not stable.** §5 shows tournaments that run debate Friday and others that run it Saturday.

Congress is a hybrid. Its content is debate (sides, bills), but its room is a speech room (many students, ranks, points per speech, one scorer for a long session). PLAN.md Q8 already placed it in Speech Events. This research agrees.

**What the calendar does change (data, not app choice):**

- **League packs know their season.** A league pack can carry the events of each meet (for example, a UIL spring pack lists Prose, Poetry, Informative and Persuasive Extemp, and says LD is judged in Tabula Debate). That is one line of data per pack, added with the pack (LANDING.md §4.2).
- **A hand-off card.** In Speech Events room setup, picking "LD", "PF" or "Policy" in a league pack opens a short card: "Judge this round in Tabula Debate" with a link to `debate.tabulaspeech.com`. Same account, no new sign-in. Tabula Debate gets the reverse card for speech events. Small, and only after v1.
- **One judging day across both apps.** A judge at a spring UIL or Missouri state meet may judge LD on Friday and Prose on Saturday, or both on one day. PLAN.md Q2 says "a round is your judging day" for hosted AI in Speech Events. Whether one day of rounds should cover hosted AI in both apps is a pricing and cloud question. It is listed in §6 for the cloud session, not decided here.

## 5. Friday and Saturday: real examples

"Debate (and often Congress) on Friday, speech on Saturday" is common. It is not a rule.

| Tournament | Layout | Source (confidence) |
|---|---|---|
| Missouri MSHSAA state 2025 | Friday debate prelims to semis; Saturday speech prelims and finals, debate finals | [MSHSAA schedule](https://www.mshsaa.org/resources/Activities/SpeechandDebate/General%20Schedule.pdf) (primary) |
| NSDA New England district 2026 | Congress on its own Saturday; PF and LD online Friday evening; speech, Big Questions, PF and LD the next Saturday | [district invitation](https://s3.amazonaws.com/tabroom-files/tourns/38074/postings/66774/2026NEDistrictQualifiersInvitation.pdf) (primary) |
| NSDA Chesapeake district 2026 | Everything on one Saturday; Congress uses one 3-hour session and a 3-hour final | [district invitation](https://s3.amazonaws.com/tabroom-files/tourns/38495/postings/70189/ChesapeakeNSDADistrictsInvitation2026.pdf) (primary) |
| Invitationals in Mississippi, Washington, California | Debate and Congress start Friday; speech Saturday | Tabroom pages via search summaries (secondary) |
| Invitationals in Idaho and South Dakota | Speech Friday; debate Saturday | Tabroom pages via search summaries (secondary) |

**For the app:** a room or round never needs to know the day of the week. The Rooms list groups by tournament day (ARCHITECTURE.md §5), which already covers a Friday-plus-Saturday tournament.
