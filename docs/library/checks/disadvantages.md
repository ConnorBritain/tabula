## Which part is missing?

The neg reads this DA in the 1NC.

```
1NC  DA: Spending
U: budget balanced now
L: plan costs $50B
I: cuts to school lunch -> more hunger
```

In words: the budget is balanced now; the plan costs 50 billion dollars; the impact is cuts to school lunch, so more hunger.

Which part of this DA is missing?

- [ ] Uniqueness
- [ ] Link
- [x] Internal link
- [ ] Impact

**Why:** The link says the plan costs money. The impact is about cuts. Nothing says that a new cost *forces* cuts. That step is the internal link. The 2AC can point at the gap: "No internal link."

## Is it happening now?

The neg reads this DA in the 1NC.

```
1NC  DA: Jobs
L: plan closes old power plants
IL: closed plants -> layoffs
I: towns lose jobs, families hurt
```

In words: the plan closes old power plants; closed plants lead to layoffs; towns lose jobs and families are hurt.

Which part of this DA is missing?

- [x] Uniqueness
- [ ] Link
- [ ] Internal link
- [ ] Impact

**Why:** Nothing says that jobs in those towns are safe now. If layoffs will happen anyway, the plan does not cause them. The neg needs uniqueness: the bad thing is not happening now. Without it, the 2AC can say "non-unique."

## Why is it bad?

The neg reads this DA in the 1NC.

```
1NC  DA: Trade
U: trade talks on track now
L: plan angers trade partner
IL: angry partner -> talks fail
```

In words: trade talks are on track now; the plan angers the trade partner; an angry partner makes the talks fail.

Which part of this DA is missing?

- [ ] Uniqueness
- [ ] Link
- [ ] Internal link
- [x] Impact

**Why:** The DA says the talks fail, but never says why that is bad. The impact is the harm at the end, the reason the judge should care. Without it, the judge has nothing to weigh against the aff's advantages.

## What does the plan do?

The neg reads this DA in the 1NC.

```
1NC  DA: Taxes
U: no new taxes this yr
IL: new taxes -> slow economy
I: slow economy -> lost jobs
```

In words: there are no new taxes this year; new taxes slow the economy; a slow economy means lost jobs.

Which part of this DA is missing?

- [ ] Uniqueness
- [x] Link
- [ ] Internal link
- [ ] Impact

**Why:** Nothing connects the plan to new taxes. The link is the part that says the plan starts the chain. Without it, the DA is about taxes in general, not about this plan. The 2AC can say "no link."
