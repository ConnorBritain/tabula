## Already fixing it

The aff plan: the federal government requires smoke alarms in all rental homes. The aff says renters die in fires because many rental homes have no alarm.

The neg says: "Most states passed new alarm laws last year. The status quo is already fixing the problem." Which stock issue does this answer attack?

- [ ] Harms
- [x] Inherency
- [ ] Solvency
- [ ] Topicality

**Why:** Inherency asks whether the problem will last without the plan. The neg says the world now is already fixing it. If that is true, the plan adds little.

## Will it work?

The aff plan: the federal government requires safety checks on all school buses. The aff says the checks will cut bus accidents.

The neg says: "There are no inspectors to do the checks. The plan will not work." Which stock issue does this answer attack?

- [ ] Harms
- [ ] Inherency
- [x] Solvency
- [ ] Significance

**Why:** Solvency asks whether the plan fixes the problem. The neg does not say the problem is fake. It says the plan will not fix it.

## Does it fit?

The topic says the federal government should change its policy on education. The aff plan: the federal government funds new roads in rural areas.

The neg says: "Roads are not education. The plan is off the topic." Which stock issue does this answer attack?

- [ ] Harms
- [ ] Inherency
- [ ] Solvency
- [x] Topicality

**Why:** Topicality asks whether the plan fits the resolution, the topic statement. It is usually argued on its own sheet, apart from the case.

## Is the problem real?

The aff plan: the federal government bans a certain cleaning product in schools. The aff says the product makes students sick.

The neg says: "Their only card is about factory workers who use the product all day. There is no proof that it makes any student sick." Which stock issue does this answer attack?

- [x] Harms
- [ ] Inherency
- [ ] Solvency
- [ ] Topicality

**Why:** Harms asks whether there is a real problem now. The neg says the aff has not proven that students are hurt at all. That attacks the harm, not the plan.

## Too small to matter

The aff plan: the federal government puts a warning label on a certain toy. The aff says children get hurt by the toy.

The neg says: "Their own card says about ten children a year get a small cut. That is too small to care about." Which stock issue does this answer attack?

- [x] Significance
- [ ] Inherency
- [ ] Solvency
- [ ] Topicality

**Why:** Significance asks whether the problem is big enough to matter. The neg agrees the harm happens, but says it is too small. Many judges fold significance into harms. Traditional judges often treat it as its own item, and a stock issues judge may vote neg if the neg wins it.
