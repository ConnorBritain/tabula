## Clear or fast?

Speaker A reads many cards very fast. You cannot understand most of the tags, and the speech jumps between arguments. Speaker B reads fewer cards, speaks clearly, follows a roadmap, and explains why each argument matters.

Which speaker usually deserves higher points?

- [ ] Speaker A, because they read more evidence
- [x] Speaker B, because the speech was clear, organized and explained
- [ ] Both the same, because both spoke for the full time
- [ ] Speaker A, because speed always shows skill

**Why:** Points reward clarity, organization and explanation. Speed is fine only if the judge can still follow it. If you could not understand the tags, those arguments did not help you decide.

## Points and the win

Team X wins the round. But a debater on Team Y, the losing team, gave the best speeches in the round.

Can the debater on the losing team get the highest points?

- [x] Yes, because points rate each speaker apart from the decision
- [ ] No, because the winning team must always get higher points
- [ ] Only if the other judge agrees
- [ ] Only if the tournament has no ballot

**Why:** Points are separate from the win. A debater on the losing team can earn the highest points. Some tournaments forbid or flag a "low-point win", where the whole winning team has fewer points. Follow your tournament's rule.

## Style you do not like

Speaker C talks in a calm, quiet style that you do not prefer. But the speech is clear, answers every argument, and makes smart choices in the rebuttal. Speaker D has a style you like, but drops two key arguments.

Which speaker usually deserves higher points?

- [x] Speaker C, because the debating was clear and smart
- [ ] Speaker D, because you liked the style
- [ ] Both the same, because style and strategy cancel out
- [ ] Neither, because style should not get points

**Why:** Do not punish style alone. Points rate clarity, strategy and explanation. Dropping key arguments is a real weakness. A style you do not prefer is not.

## What points rate

You have heard that one debater has a strong record and is well known on the circuit.

What should your points for that debater rate?

- [ ] Their record and how well known they are
- [x] How well they debated in this round
- [ ] Who they are as a person
- [ ] How their team did at the last tournament

**Why:** Points rate this round only. They rate the debating, not the person, and not a reputation or a record.
