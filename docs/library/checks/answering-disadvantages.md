## Which answers are turns?

The 1NC reads this DA (disadvantage). The plan takes money from the city road budget. So the city builds fewer new roads. Fewer new roads means worse traffic.

The 2AC can make many answers. Which answers are turns: answers that, if the aff wins them, make the DA a reason to vote aff? Pick all that apply.

- [x] "Link turn: the plan frees money for road work, so the city builds more roads, not fewer."
- [x] "Impact turn: fewer new roads is good. New roads bring more cars and more pollution (Analyst 22)."
- [ ] "Non-unique: the city already stopped building new roads this year."
- [ ] "Impact defense: traffic gets only a little worse, and only after many years."

**Why:** A link turn says the plan does the opposite and prevents the harm. An impact turn says the harm is actually good. Both are offense. Non-unique and impact defense are defense: they only make the DA smaller. They do not give the judge a reason to vote aff.

## Why add a turn?

A 2AC block on a DA has only defense: non-unique, no link and impact defense.

Why do most blocks also try to include a turn?

- [ ] Because defense answers do not count on the flow
- [x] Because defense only makes the DA smaller, and some judges think a DA keeps some risk
- [ ] Because the 1AR may extend only turns
- [ ] Because a block with a turn cannot lose

**Why:** Defense alone rarely brings a DA to zero. Some judges think almost every DA keeps some risk. Judges differ on whether strong defense can bring a DA to zero. A turn can make the DA a reason to vote aff, so the aff has something to win, not only something to shrink.

## Two turns on one DA

The 1NC reads a DA. The plan slows job growth, and slow job growth hurts families. The 2AC says:

```
2AC
1. L/T: plan speeds job growth
2. Impact turn: slow job growth
   is good for families
```

In words: answer 1 says the plan speeds job growth. Answer 2 says slow job growth is good.

What is the problem with these two answers together?

- [ ] Nothing. Two turns are twice as strong as one.
- [x] It is a double turn: it says the plan stops something good
- [ ] An impact turn is not allowed in the 2AC
- [ ] A link turn needs a non-unique answer, or it does not count

**Why:** If the plan speeds job growth, and slow growth is good, then the plan takes away a good thing. That is a double turn, and it helps the neg. Pick one kind of turn on each DA: link turns or impact turns, not both.

## A strong pair

The 1NC reads a DA. The plan causes power outages, and outages hurt hospitals.

Which two 2AC answers work best together as a pair?

- [x] "Non-unique: outages are already coming" and "Link turn: the plan prevents outages"
- [ ] "Link turn: the plan prevents outages" and "Impact turn: outages are good"
- [ ] "Non-unique: outages are already coming" and "Impact defense: outages are short"

**Why:** Non-unique says the harm is coming. The link turn says the plan stops it. Together they say the plan makes things better, so the DA becomes a reason to vote aff. A link turn plus an impact turn is a double turn. Non-unique plus impact defense is only defense, so it can make the DA smaller but cannot turn it.

## What the 1AR extends

The neg answered some 2AC answers in the block, but not all.

```
2AC                 Block
1. N/U: harm      New card: harm
   coming now       not coming yet
2. L/T: plan      (no answer)
   stops harm
3. Impact D:      Strong new card:
   harm is small    harm is big
4. Case outweighs Their harm is
                    faster
```

In words: the block answered the non-unique, impact defense and case outweighs points. The block did not answer the link turn.

The 1AR has little time. What should it do? Pick all that apply.

- [x] Extend the link turn, and say the neg dropped it
- [x] Keep weighing: say why the case outweighs any risk left
- [ ] Extend all four answers with the same time on each
- [ ] Add a new impact turn that was not in the 2AC

**Why:** A dropped link turn is a big gain, so extend it. The 2AR needs a reason that the case outweighs, so keep weighing. You do not have to extend every answer. Let weak ones go on purpose. A brand-new turn in the 1AR is not an extension of the 2AC block.
