Guided tracks

A track is a short course: library pages in order, with a quick check at the foot of each page.

  • Your progress: it is saved only in your browser. There is no sign-up.
  • New parent judge (draft, in review): You can judge a fair round today. 6 steps.
  • Novice debater (draft, in review): You can run and answer the core arguments. 14 steps.
  • Intro to the K (draft, in review): You can explain and answer a basic kritik. 7 steps.
  • Theory basics (draft, in review): You can read, run and answer a basic theory argument. 6 steps.
  • Judging well (draft, in review): You can decide close rounds and explain them well. 7 steps.

Your progress

Your progress is saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent to anyone. On a shared computer, the next person can see it, so clear it when you finish.
