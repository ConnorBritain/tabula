Used when a key you connected does the transcription. Free browser transcription always uses Chrome's default mic and its own sound settings, so the mic choice and the room/Zoom setting do not apply to it, and it shows no level meter.

Tips for a live room
  • Put the laptop between the podium and the timekeeper, about 1–2 m from the speaker, screen facing you.
  • Keep it off the desk you type on hardest; keyboard taps get picked up.
  • For big rooms, a USB or clip-on mic near the podium helps a lot.
  • Run the 5-second test with someone talking at the podium before the 1AC.
