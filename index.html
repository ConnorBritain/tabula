Reading level

How the AI writes the Live Eval, analysis, decision and feedback. Plain is easier for students and parents, and good for Tabroom comments. Each level keeps its own saved answers, so you can switch back and forth. The verdict does not change, only the wording. The same switch is in the top bar.

Round documents

Add speech docs or case files (PDF, Word .docx, or text). Claude reads them for the live flow and the decision. Pick the speech each doc belongs to: Claude maps it onto that speech's flow column right away, and fills gaps in the transcript from it (marked [doc]). Docs stay in this browser until you start a new round.

Connect AI

Paste a key from any provider below. The app works out which one it is and checks that it works. Best value: an OpenAI key. It does transcription and AI, and in our test its small model flowed about as well as Claude Sonnet for about 4 cents a round.

Use a Claude or ChatGPT subscription instead of a key

Audio

Used by Gemini, OpenAI and Grok transcription. Free browser transcription always uses Chrome's default mic and its own sound settings, so the mic choice and the room/Zoom setting do not apply to it, and it shows no level meter.

Tips for a live room
  • Put the laptop between the podium and the timekeeper, about 1–2 m from the speaker, screen facing you.
  • Keep it off the desk you type on hardest; keyboard taps get picked up.
  • For big rooms, a USB or clip-on mic near the podium helps a lot.
  • Run the 5-second test with someone talking at the podium before the 1AC.

Welcome to Tabula

Timers, flow sheets and the ballot always work. AI adds live transcripts, an auto-flow, a Live Eval and analysis. How do you want to start?

Without AI you get:

  • Speech and prep timers, flow sheets, the round map (drawn by hand), the ballot and the Tabroom copy boxes.
  • Free browser transcription in Chrome (weaker on fast speech).

You will not get:

  • The AI flow, Live Eval, Impact Calculus, Paradigm Lens, auto-drawn map or decision draft.
