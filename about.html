Tabula is a free judging tool for high school policy debate. It keeps time, keeps the flow, listens to the round, and helps you write a fair, readable decision for the debaters.

How it works

1. BeforeType the teams and add the speech docs. Pick how you judge.
2. DuringRun the clock, record the speeches, and flow. The AI can flow alongside you, and your edits always win.
3. AfterRead the live evaluation, weigh it under your paradigm, and fill the ballot. Copy each box into Tabroom.

Works with an AI key (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, xAI Grok or OpenRouter), with a Claude or ChatGPT subscription (on a Mac, through a small helper that runs on your computer), or with no AI at all.

Privacy

  • Your rounds stay in your own browser. There is no Tabula account and no Tabula server that stores them.
  • When you use AI, the round is sent only to the AI provider you chose, with your own key or subscription.
  • Your AI keys stay in this browser. They are saved as plain text unless you untick "Remember keys" in Settings.
  • Audio is held on this device only until it is transcribed. Then it is deleted. Short clips go to the transcription service you chose (or Chrome's own speech service), and only the text is saved. Audio is never in a backup or a round file.
  • Rounds include students' names and voices. Please follow your league's and school's rules when you record.
